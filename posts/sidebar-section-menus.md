---
title: Sidebar section menus
date: 2026-09-18T00:00:00Z
slug: sidebar-section-menus
---

The section headers in your sidebar — Starred, Shared with me, and Collections — now have their own menus, so you can reorganize your sidebar without dragging things around.

- **Reorder sections** – right-click a header, or hover to reveal the menu button, and choose "Move up" or "Move down" to change the order sections appear in
- **Create collections faster** – the Collections header now has a quick-add button and a "New collection" menu item
- **Works with keyboard and mouse** – menus open the same way whether you're using a pointer or navigating by keyboard

Your preferred section order is remembered, and hidden sections are skipped automatically when reordering.
