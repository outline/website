---
title: Archive and deletion reasons
date: 2026-09-19T00:00:00Z
slug: archive-deletion-reasons
---

When you archive or delete a document or collection, you can now leave a short note explaining why. The reason shows up right in the notice banner, so anyone who comes across it later — including your future self — has the context without needing to track someone down.

Reasons are cleared automatically if a document or collection is restored, and this applies to any child documents that were archived or deleted along with it. It's also available to AI agents through Outline's MCP tools, so an agent that archives or deletes something on your behalf can record why.
