![Upgraded diagrams](/images/pele-diagrams.png)

Mermaid diagrams in Outline are now rendered with [Pele](https://pele.run), a small and super fast renderer that produces clean SVG diagrams. You write diagrams with the same Mermaid syntax as before, but they now load faster, look sharper, and fit naturally with the rest of your document.

- **Beautiful by default** – diagrams take their colors from Outline's theme, with tidier layouts and fewer crossing lines
- **Instant theme switching** – diagrams change immediately between light and dark mode without re-rendering, and always print in the right theme
