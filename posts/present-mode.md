![Present mode](/images/present-mode.png)

Any document can now be presented as a slideshow, straight from Outline – no need to copy your content into a separate presentation tool. Choose "Present" from the document menu, or press `⌘+Option+P`, and the document is automatically split into slides at its headings and dividers.

- **Keyboard navigation** – move between slides with the arrow keys, space, or page up/down, jump to the start or end with home and end, and press escape to exit
- **Fullscreen** – press `F` to go fullscreen, controls fade away when the mouse is idle so nothing distracts from your content
- **Interactive** – links and mentions still work while presenting, so you can jump to related documents mid-presentation
