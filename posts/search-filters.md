![Search filters](/images/search-filters.png)

Search has a redesigned set of filters, making it much easier to narrow down results in a large knowledge base. Filters now appear as a row of chips below the search box – Collection, Edited by, and Status are always available, and more can be added from the "More" menu.

- **New filters** – find documents by who created them, when they were created or published, which template they were made from, or match on titles only
- **Custom date ranges** – date filters offer quick options like past week or past month, or pick an exact range from a calendar
- **Browse without a query** – set any filter and matching documents are listed immediately, no search term needed
- **Data attributes** – On the Business plan, data attributes now appear as filter options
